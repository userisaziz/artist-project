How to get the darkest bridal mehendi stain
Ayesha Mehendi Art ✓
6 Oct 2026 · 2.4k reads
Follow

The colour of your mehendi is decided long before the paste comes off. After 600+ brides, these are the three things I tell every client the week before.

1. Book it 48 hours early

Henna keeps darkening for two days. If your mehendi is the night before the wedding, it will still be orange at the pheras.

Day 1 (left) and day 2 (right), same bride
"Keep the paste on for at least six hours. Overnight is even better."
2. Skip water for 12 hours

Scrape the dried paste off rather than washing it. A little mustard oil or balm protects the stain while it oxidises.
