1. Executive summary

[CLIENT BUSINESS NAME] wants to bring beauty and event-service professionals — mehendi artists, makeup artists, hairstylists and related services — and their customers onto a single, trusted platform. The goal is a product that feels effortless for a customer to discover and book a professional, and empowering for a professional to showcase work and get discovered.

We propose a two-sided marketplace delivered as iOS & Android mobile apps (customer and artist), plus a web admin panel for you to run the business — backed by a secure API and database. We recommend a phased launch: a focused, high-quality MVP for the December-2026 go-live, then a Phase 2 that layers in in-app payments, reviews, messaging and video-led promotion.

A head start already exists. Core foundation work is complete — phone + OTP sign-in, account sign-up, customer/artist role selection, artist onboarding and multi-role accounts. That de-risks the timeline and means December is a realistic launch date for the agreed MVP scope, not an optimistic guess.

2. Our understanding of your business

  • Two audiences. Customers want to find, compare and book trustworthy professionals quickly. Professionals want visibility, a portfolio, and a steady flow of bookings.
  • Trust is the product. Verified profiles, real portfolios and (Phase 2) reviews are what convert browsing into bookings.
  • Discovery is visual. Beauty services are chosen by look-and-feel, so photo/video-led presentation matters more than on typical marketplaces.
  • You operate the marketplace. An admin capability is needed to onboard/verify professionals, manage services/categories, view bookings and handle disputes.
  • Events matter. Booking windows, availability and lead times differ for weddings/events versus walk-in style services.

Why payments & video are Phase 2. In-app payments introduce gateway, KYC/compliance, commission and reconciliation complexity — the highest-cost, highest-risk part of a marketplace. 5. Technology approach

Stack

  • Mobile: Expo / React Native — one codebase ships iOS & Android
  • API: Python FastAPI (async), versioned REST
  • Data: PostgreSQL with Alembic-migrated schema
  • Auth: Phone + OTP, JWT access/refresh with rotation
  • Admin: Web panel (React) over the same API
  • Media: Cloud object storage + CDN for images

Quality & operations

  • Automated tests on backend & app; code review throughout
  • Staging environment for your review before each release
  • Secure-by-default: hashed tokens/OTP, role-based access
  • Hosting: [cloud provider] — figures quoted in Section 7
  • Data ownership & export rest with you (see Terms)

Reusability note. The authentication, roles, and onboarding layers are already built and tested for this project. That proven foundation is folded into the MVP at no re-development cost.
